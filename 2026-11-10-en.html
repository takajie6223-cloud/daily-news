Front Page · U.S. Midterm Elections

Trump's Approval Falls to 32%, Near a Career Low

A new Reuters/Ipsos poll finds worries about rising prices still weighing on the White House, after Democrats swept both chambers of Congress in the November 3 midterm elections and handed Republicans a heavy defeat.

Source Reuters

A Reuters/Ipsos poll of 1,705 U.S. adults conducted November 6-9 found that only 32% approve of Trump's performance, unchanged from the two previous polls in late October and early November and near the lowest level of his political career. Voters' concerns about rising prices were a central reason for the Republican defeat in the midterms.

Democrats not only recorded their largest House victory since at least 2008, but also flipped Senate seats in traditionally conservative states including Kansas, South Carolina and Texas. With some races still undecided, Democrats have secured control of both chambers, reshaping the room Trump has to govern for the rest of his term.

No. 05 · China Prices

China's October PPI Jumps 5.7%, Fastest in Nearly Four and a Half Years, as CPI Cools to 0.7%

China's producer price index rose 5.7% year on year in October, data from the National Bureau of Statistics showed on Monday, up from 5.0% in September and above the 5.3% forecast in a Reuters poll — the fastest pace since June 2022 — as higher oil prices raised manufacturing costs. Consumer prices rose just 0.7%, down from 0.9% in September and below the expected 0.8%, while core inflation eased to 0.8%, suggesting weak domestic demand is preventing costs from fully reaching consumers.

Source: Reuters · Read original

No. 10 · Model Release

Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2, 30% Faster and 20% Cheaper

Anthropic on Monday unveiled Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2, targeting coding, reasoning and AI-agent tasks, with outputs up to 30% faster than the 5.1 versions and prices cut to $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output tokens. Fable 5.2 is available to all users, while Mythos 5.2 is restricted to organisations approved through its cybersecurity and life-sciences verification programmes. The launch comes just before the company's IPO roadshow.

No. 11 · AI Chips

Google Unveils TPU 8tx and 8i Max, Stepping Up Its Challenge to Nvidia

Google on Monday introduced two new versions of its in-house tensor processing units: the TPU 8tx, a confidential-computing system for governments and regulated industries, and the TPU 8i Max, for AI applications needing very large context windows. Both are redesigned versions of its eighth-generation TPUs rather than entirely new chips. Alphabet shares rose more than 3% in early trading after the announcement.

No. 12 · Robot AI

Nvidia Unveils Robot AI Model GR00T 2, Reaching 95% Success After Just Five Demonstrations

Nvidia on Monday introduced a new robotics AI model, GR00T 2. In tests on 50 tasks, it achieved an average success rate of 71% after seeing one demonstration per task, rising to 95% after five demonstrations. The model was trained on robot data and 100,000 hours of first-person video, and Foxconn is applying it to automate parts of its server production.
