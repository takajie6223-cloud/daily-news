Front Page · New U.S. Tariffs

U.S. Proposes New Tariffs on 14 Trading Partners, With China Hit Hardest

The Trump administration on Wednesday unveiled the results of its Section 301 investigation, proposing new tariffs on 14 major trading partners — including China — to combat what it calls structural excess industrial capacity. The move deepened a market already rattled by the AI sell-off.

Source Reuters

Reuters reported that Washington blames China most for flooding world markets with cheap exports, and that China would therefore face the highest rates. The proposed measures target sectors the U.S. says suffer from excess capacity; exact rates and lists are still to follow, but the White House has made clear it does not intend to retreat from its protectionist course. U.S. stocks swung sharply on the news: the Nasdaq fell 2% on Wednesday and is now almost 20% below its early-October record high, flirting with bear-market territory. Asian markets also slid, with South Korea's KOSPI down another 6% and Japan's Nikkei off almost 3%. Investors weighed the tariff risk alongside Wednesday's U.S. October PCE inflation data.

来源 路透社
