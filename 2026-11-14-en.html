Front Page · Nvidia Leadership Change

Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO; Kress Takes Over as Interim Chief

The founder who led Nvidia for 33 years stepped down abruptly on Thursday as the board began searching for a successor; Nvidia shares plunged as much as 14% intraday before closing down 4.3%.

Source Reuters·USA Today

Nvidia announced on November 12 that Jensen Huang had resigned as president, chief executive officer and a member of the board, effective immediately; chief financial officer Colette Kress will serve as interim chief executive while remaining CFO, and Harvey Jones has been named board chair. The company gave no reason for the resignation in its statement. The board had earlier announced an independent investigation into whether Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence operation, following reports about his relationship with a businesswoman U.S. intelligence agencies suspect of spying for China, and with a direct report.

The market reaction was sharp: Nvidia shares fell as much as 14% intraday on Thursday to their lowest in nearly 16 months, before paring the loss to 4.3% at the close, at $166.19. The stock is now down about 30% from its October 6 high, having shed almost $2 trillion in market value. President Donald Trump suggested former Apple chief Tim Cook should take over. Nvidia is due to report earnings on November 17, when leadership uncertainty and the outlook for AI demand will be in focus.
