Front Page · AI Bubble Fears Ease

CoreWeave Results Ignite AI Rally as Nasdaq Jumps 1.6%

CoreWeave, once seen as a bellwether for AI-bubble fears, beat expectations and pointed to adjusted profitability in the first half of 2028, lifting AI cloud stocks across the board as Nvidia rose about 5% in a day.

Source Reuters

CoreWeave reported third-quarter revenue of $3.66 billion, above the $3.55 billion analysts had expected on average and up sharply from $1.37 billion a year earlier. The company also raised its full-year revenue forecast and said it would not tap the unsecured debt market, arguing that cash flow from existing contracts would cover current and future debt repayments — easing fears about debt-fuelled AI infrastructure expansion.

U.S. stocks rallied across the board on Wednesday: the Nasdaq Composite rose 1.6% to 25,466.19, the S&P 500 gained 1.31% to 7,619.38, and the Dow added 0.49% to 52,486.49. But inflation data remained hot and expectations of a December Federal Reserve rate rise linger, so whether the AI rebound can last will depend on this week's jobs and producer-price data.

Ukraine Talks in Abu Dhabi End With Deal on Major Prisoner Swap

U.S.-led talks between Ukraine and Russia ran for two days in Abu Dhabi, the first meeting between the sides since February. No. 07 · Inflation and Central Banks

Hot U.S. Inflation Puts a 50-Basis-Point December Hike on the Table, Waller Says

U.S. consumer prices rose 0.4% month on month in October, above the 0.3% expected, while core CPI rose 0.3% for a third straight month. Federal Reserve Governor Christopher Waller said earlier the same day in London that he leaned toward a 25-basis-point hike in December, but that a 50-basis-point move should be considered if core inflation stayed around 0.3%. Markets priced in roughly a 90% chance of a December hike, and the 10-year Treasury yield rose to near 5%.

Source: Reuters · Bloomberg · Read original

Anthropic Launches IPO Roadshow Seeking Up to $64.4 Billion at Valuation of Up to About $1.4 Trillion

Google announced on November 10 that Gemini 4 Carbon is now available to paying developers, enterprises and Ultra subscribers. It is the company's strongest model yet, built for long-horizon agentic workflows, complex software engineering, scientific reasoning and cybersecurity. Its introductory price matches Argon — $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens — and doubles after November 30.

No. 12 · Nvidia in Turmoil

Huang Affair Deepens: FBI Probe, Board Hires Law Firm, Investors File Class Action

Reported ties between Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang and a woman U.S. authorities concluded was a Chinese intelligence asset have prompted an FBI investigation, calls for congressional hearings and an independent board review. The board has hired the Wachtell law firm to assist, and a proposed investor class action was filed on Monday in federal court in San Jose, California. Huang admitted on Sunday that he had been unfaithful to his wife, without giving details.

No. 13 · AI Earnings

OpenAI's Quarterly Revenue Tops $10 Billion for the First Time, but Operating Loss Widens to $10.9 Billion

OpenAI's third-quarter revenue reached about $10 billion, up nearly 50% from the previous quarter and above $10 billion for the first time, the Wall Street Journal and Bloomberg reported — but its adjusted operating loss widened from $10.1 billion to $10.9 billion. Rival Anthropic, by contrast, reported about $17 billion in revenue for the same period with a loss of just $567 million, a stark gap in enterprise-market efficiency.
