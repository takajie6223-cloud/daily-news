Front Page · Trilateral Summit

Trump, Xi and Putin Meet in Shenzhen and Issue a Five-Paragraph “Peace Declaration”

The leaders of the United States, China and Russia held their first-ever trilateral meeting, discussing Ukraine, nuclear arms control and artificial intelligence. The declaration was more symbolic than substantive, while European allies met urgently in Berlin to coordinate a response.

Source Reuters

The three leaders met for less than two hours on Thursday at the Kylin Villa compound in Shenzhen before issuing a five-paragraph “peace declaration.” Trump said before the meeting that the gathering itself was already a victory; he also discussed trade and critical minerals with Xi and proposed nuclear arms reductions by the United States, Russia and China, without offering details. Putin rejected suggestions that the meeting was meant to repeat the 1945 Yalta Conference or carve the world into spheres of influence. Substantive results were limited: Ukraine was raised, but no concrete breakthrough emerged. Xi made no mention of Ukraine in his opening remarks, while Trump said he hoped to work with China and Russia to end the fighting. In Europe, there were concerns that decisions affecting European security could be taken without European input. Ukraine’s European allies met in Berlin the same day as the “Coalition of the Willing,” reaffirming legally binding security guarantees to take effect after a ceasefire.

World Court

World Court Rules Myanmar Committed Genocide Against the Rohingya

The United Nations’ International Court of Justice ruled on Thursday that mass killings and widespread sexual violence by Myanmar’s security forces against Rohingya Muslims in 2017 amounted to state-sponsored genocide. It was only the fourth time since the Nazi Holocaust that an international court has found the legal threshold for genocide to have been met. The ruling is final and cannot be appealed, although the court has limited means of enforcement; the case was brought by Gambia in 2019, after more than 730,000 Rohingya fled to Bangladesh.

Source: Reuters · Read original
