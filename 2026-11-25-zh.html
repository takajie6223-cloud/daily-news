Front Page · The Nvidia Saga

Huang Returns to Nvidia After Reinstatement as Trump Intervention Stirs Wall Street Debate

Just 11 days after resigning, Jensen Huang returned to Nvidia's top job following Donald Trump's personal intervention, sending shares up 31% on Monday; the rally faded fast on Tuesday, leaving governance, regulatory and chip-export questions unresolved.

Source Reuters

Nvidia's board announced on November 23 that it had reinstated Jensen Huang as chief executive, president and a director. Trump said the return would not have happened without his personal intervention. After the announcement, the Nasdaq jumped about 7% on Monday, the Philadelphia Semiconductor Index rose 14%, and Nvidia added more than $900 billion in market value in a single day. The euphoria lasted only a day. Nvidia shares fell 6% on Tuesday and remain about 37% below their October 6 high. Huang still faces multiple investigations by U.S. regulators and Congress, covering possible chip diversion to China, accounting and financing questions, and antitrust concerns. A Reuters commentary said the intervention raised unprecedented questions about the line between Washington and Wall Street.

Huawei Convicted on 10 Counts in U.S. Criminal Trial

No. 07 · Singapore Economy

Singapore's Q3 GDP Grows 6.1% as Full-Year Forecast Raised to Around 6%

Singapore's Ministry of Energy, Trade and Industry said on Tuesday that the economy grew 6.1% year on year in the third quarter, above the 5.4% advance estimate and the 5.6% forecast in a Reuters poll, as AI-related demand lifted wholesale trade and manufacturing. The government raised its 2026 growth forecast to around 6%, from 4.5% to 5.5%, but expects growth to slow to 2.5% to 4.5% in 2027.
