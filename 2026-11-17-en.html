Front Page · China–U.S.–Russia Trilateral Meeting

Xi, Trump and Putin to Hold First-Ever Trilateral Meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday; Kremlin Says It Will Have a Major Impact on Global Affairs

The three leaders will meet on the sidelines of the APEC summit, with Ukraine and nuclear arms control expected to top the agenda; Trump also said he might meet Kim Jong Un in China.

Source Reuters

The Kremlin said on Monday that Russian President Vladimir Putin, Chinese President Xi Jinping and U.S. President Donald Trump are scheduled to hold a trilateral meeting in Shenzhen on Thursday, at Xi's official residence. Kremlin aide Yuri Ushakov said it would be the first meeting of its kind and would have a major impact on global affairs. China's Foreign Ministry confirmed the same day that the meeting will take place on the sidelines of the APEC summit, describing it as the first face-to-face meeting of the three heads of state. The White House said last week that Trump would raise efforts to reduce nuclear arms at the meeting. Trump said on Monday that he planned to discuss Russia's war in Ukraine with Xi and Putin in Shenzhen, and that he might meet North Korean leader Kim Jong Un in China. The Kremlin said Putin could meet Kim if he also came to China for the APEC summit. This year's APEC Economic Leaders' Meeting will be held in Shenzhen on November 18–19 under the theme of building an Asia-Pacific community to prosper together.

No. 11 · Nvidia

Nvidia Launches Nemotron for Science: Open Science Models Plus a Nearly 50,000-Question Chemistry Dataset

Nvidia introduced Nemotron for Science on Monday at the SC26 conference in Chicago, bringing together its open Nemotron 3.5 Super VL model, a new open chemistry dataset and post-training recipes to help researchers build specialised agents for scientific discovery. The new LitmusBench v0.2 dataset contains nearly 50,000 questions for training and evaluating language models on chemistry reasoning. The model and dataset are available on Hugging Face, and Argonne National Laboratory is integrating Nemotron into a system for designing and evaluating protein-based therapeutic candidates.

Anthropic IPO Enters the Countdown: $70–$78 a Share, Valuing the Company at About $1.3 Trillion

Anthropic has priced its IPO: 825 million Class A shares at $70 to $78 each, raising up to about $64.4 billion, plus a $26 billion private placement that brings the total to as much as $90.4 billion. No. 14 · AI Security

U.S. and Nine Allies Sign Declaration to Jointly Protect AI Technology and Critical Infrastructure

The United States and nine of its allies have signed a declaration to coordinate efforts to secure the technologies and critical infrastructure that AI supply chains depend on, the U.S. State Department said late on Sunday. The signatories are the United States, Australia, India, Israel, Japan, South Korea, Qatar, Singapore, the United Arab Emirates and the United Kingdom. The San Francisco Declaration says economic security underpins national security and calls for shared protection of sensitive technologies and AI infrastructure. Germany, Sweden, the Netherlands and the European Commission are set to sign next week.

No. 15 · Fed and AI

Fed Chair Warsh: AI Will Have a 'Significant Impact', With Three Scenarios in Play

Federal Reserve Chair Kevin Warsh said on Monday that emerging technologies will have a significant impact on the economy, but the scenarios range from rising growth with broadly shared gains to higher unemployment and a potential slowdown if anticipated investment returns prove disappointing. Speaking at a Fed conference on AI in San Francisco, Warsh referred to artificial intelligence as "super intelligence" and said it could prove distinct from earlier technologies as a force to "supercharge idea generation." He also warned that the risks, including AI use by "bad actors," could be "highly disruptive and economically costly."

美国国务院周日表示，美国与澳大利亚、印度、以色列、日本、韩国、卡塔尔、新加坡、阿联酋和英国签署《旧金山宣言》，协调保护 AI 供应链所依赖的技术与关键基础设施。 宣言称经济安全是国家安全的基础，并呼吁共同保护敏感技术与 AI 基础设施。德国、瑞典、荷兰与欧盟委员会预计下周签署该协议。

第 15 条 · 美联储与 AI

美联储主席沃什：AI 将带来“重大影响”，三种情景并存

美联储主席沃什周一在旧金山的美联储 AI 会议上表示，新兴技术将对经济产生重大影响，但前景从生产率提升、收益广泛共享，到失业上升、投资回报不及预期导致放缓，三种情景并存。 沃什将 AI 称为“超级智能”，称其可能“超级加速创意产生”。他同时警告，AI 被“不良行为者”利用的风险也可能“极具破坏性且代价高昂”。
