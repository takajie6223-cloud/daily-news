Wall Street Roars Back: Nasdaq Jumps 2.3% as Dow Posts Best Week Since 2022

In a shortened post-Thanksgiving session, AI stocks led a broad rally, with all three major indexes posting big weekly gains and recouping part of last week's rout.

Source Reuters · Investopedia

On Friday, November 27, the Nasdaq Composite rose 2.31% to close at 24,129.50, the S&P 500 gained 1.60% to 7,297.49, and the Dow Jones Industrial Average advanced 1.19% to 50,780.64. For the week, the Nasdaq rose 6.7%, the S&P 500 5.6% and the Dow 5.5% — the Dow's best week since October 2022. Chip stocks led the rally, with Micron and Marvell each up more than 7%, while Anthropic surged 12% back above its IPO price. The rebound has not erased November's losses. Reuters notes that, as of this week, the S&P 500 remained about 8% below its October 6 record high, with the Nasdaq down more than 14%. The next tests come next week: the November jobs report and the Federal Reserve's December meeting will shape rate expectations and the direction of the AI trade.
