Front Page · U.S. Inflation and the Fed

Hotter-Than-Expected U.S. Inflation All but Locks In a December Fed Hike

October CPI rose 0.4% month on month and core CPI 0.3%, both above forecasts, while Fed Governor Christopher Waller has raised the possibility of a 50-basis-point hike in December.

Source Reuters·Investopedia

Data released by the U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics on November 10 showed the consumer price index rose 0.4% in October from the previous month, while core CPI rose 0.3% — each 0.1 percentage point above market forecasts. The bureau did not publish year-on-year figures because of data-collection issues, but a Reuters poll of 101 economists found the Federal Reserve is expected to raise rates by 25 basis points in December, taking the federal funds target range to 4.00%–4.25%, with most respondents expecting that to be the final hike of this cycle.

Before the data, Fed Governor Christopher Waller said in London that the Fed should raise rates in December even if October inflation cooled, and should consider a 50-basis-point move if core CPI rose by 0.3% or more. After the release, markets priced in a December hike still more firmly, the 10-year Treasury yield climbed to 4.98%, and all three major U.S. stock indexes closed lower, with the Nasdaq leading the decline.

美国10月通胀超预期，美联储12月加息几成定局

10月CPI环比上涨0.4%，核心CPI上涨0.3%，均高于预期；美联储理事沃勒已放话，不排除12月加息50个基点。

来源 路透社·Investopedia

美国劳工部11月10日公布的数据显示，10月消费者价格指数（CPI）环比上涨0.4%，核心CPI上涨0.3%，各比市场预期高出0.1个百分点。由于统计口径问题，劳工部未公布同比数据，但路透社对101位经济学家的调查显示，美联储料将在12月再加息25个基点，把联邦基金利率目标区间推高至4.00%—4.25%，多数受访者认为这将是本轮周期的最后一次加息。

通胀数据出炉前，沃勒已在伦敦表示，即使10月通胀有所降温，美联储也应在12月加息；若核心CPI涨幅达到0.3%或更高，则应考虑加息50个基点。数据公布后，市场对12月加息的定价进一步升温，10年期美债收益率升至4.98%，美股三大指数全线收跌，纳斯达克跌幅居前。
