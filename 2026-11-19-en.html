Front Page · Trilateral Summit

Xi, Trump and Putin to Hold First Trilateral Meeting in Shenzhen Today

The leaders of the world's three nuclear powers meet for the first time, with nuclear arms control and strategic stability in focus; with no agreed agenda beforehand, expectations of a breakthrough are low.

Source Reuters

Chinese President Xi Jinping, U.S. President Donald Trump and Russian President Vladimir Putin will hold their first-ever trilateral meeting in Shenzhen today, on the sidelines of the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation (APEC) summit. The Kremlin said about an hour has been allocated for the three-way talks, which follow a plenary session; Trump will also hold a bilateral meeting and working lunch with Xi, and meet Putin briefly afterwards, though no full-scale bilateral talks are planned. The White House says the talks will focus on nuclear non-proliferation and arms reduction, after the New START treaty — the last major nuclear arms-control agreement between Washington and Moscow — expired in February. With the three sides far apart on Ukraine, Iran and trade, and no jointly agreed agenda, analysts expect little more than a striking photograph; U.S. lawmakers have meanwhile urged Trump to press Xi on detained Americans.

Nvidia Revenue More Than Doubles to a Record in First Post-Huang Earnings Report

Nvidia's fiscal third-quarter revenue rose 103% from a year earlier to $115.8 billion, beating the $109.1 billion consensus, with data-centre revenue up 110% to $107.6 billion and adjusted earnings of $2.68 a share.
