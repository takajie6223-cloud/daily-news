Front Page · Nvidia Leadership Change

Jensen Huang Resigns as Nvidia CEO, Ending 33 Years at the Helm

Nvidia's board announced on November 12 that Jensen Huang had resigned immediately as president, chief executive and director, with CFO Colette Kress named interim CEO. The departure of the AI era's most iconic leader shook global chip and AI stocks.

Source Reuters

Nvidia said Huang had resigned as president, chief executive and board member, effective immediately; Colette Kress, chief financial officer since 2013, becomes interim CEO while retaining her finance role, and the board has begun a search covering internal and external candidates, naming Harvey C. Jones as its first-ever board chair. The statement gave no reason for the resignation, but the board had already opened an independent investigation into whether Huang had been targeted by a foreign intelligence operation.

Nvidia shares fell more than 10% in premarket trading and closed down 4.34% at $166.19, while AMD and Broadcom each rose nearly 6%, as markets repriced the AI supply chain. Nvidia still plans to report third-quarter results on November 17, and President Donald Trump publicly suggested former Apple CEO Tim Cook as Huang's successor.

U.S. Fiscal Policy

U.S. October Budget Deficit Hits a Record $404 Billion

The U.S. Treasury said on November 12 that the deficit for the first month of fiscal 2027 reached $404 billion, up $120 billion, or 42%, from a year earlier, as receipts fell 9% to $369 billion and outlays hit a record October level of $773 billion. Net customs collections fell 39% year on year, a major drag on revenue.

SoftBank Swings to a $7 Billion Quarterly Loss as Its AI Bet Bites

SoftBank swung to a net loss of about ¥1.12 trillion (about $7 billion) for July-September, its first quarterly loss in nearly two years, driven by a roughly $3.7 billion writedown on advance payments for U.S. AI data-centre power equipment and financing costs that more than doubled to ¥462 billion. Its OpenAI stake was still valued at $99.6 billion.
