Wall Street's 'Black Friday': S&P 500 and Dow Both Slide Into Correction Territory

The AI trade unravels into a global sell-off: the S&P 500 falls for a seventh straight session, the Nasdaq turns negative for the year, and Asia-Pacific shares suffer their worst week since 2020.

Source Reuters · The Wall Street Journal

On Friday, November 20, US stocks suffered a sharp sell-off: the S&P 500 fell 3.7% to 6,910.39, its seventh straight daily loss, officially entering correction territory; the Nasdaq Composite plunged 5.28% to 22,617.73, wiping out its gains for the year; and the Dow Jones Industrial Average dropped 949.73 points (-1.93%) to 48,147.33. Nvidia tumbled about 14% in a single session, and Anthropic, fresh from its record-breaking listing, fell 9.1% to $88.30. The rout spread worldwide: Asia-Pacific shares suffered their worst week since 2020, with the MSCI Asia-Pacific ex-Japan index down 8% for the week, Taiwanese shares down 17% for their worst week since 1990, and Japan's Nikkei falling about 5% in a day. Worries over lofty AI valuations and the payoff from massive infrastructure spending, together with rising bets on a December Federal Reserve rate hike and turmoil at Nvidia, drove investors toward safety; consumer staples was the only S&P 500 sector to finish in positive territory.

COP31 Talks Collapse as Fossil-Fuel Rift Sinks Climate Summit

China Cuts Margin Ratio to 80% in Fresh Market-Support Push

China's Shanghai, Shenzhen and Beijing stock exchanges announced on November 22 that, with approval from the securities regulator, the minimum margin ratio for buying shares on credit will fall to 80% from 100% starting November 30, reversing a tightening imposed in January. Fees will also be cut, and Shenzhen plans market makers for ChiNext. Outstanding margin loans have fallen more than 20% from their late-June peak to about 2.2 trillion yuan.

Voting closed on Sunday in Hong Kong's 2026 Election Committee Subsector Ordinary Elections, with 5,640 registered voters casting ballots — a record turnout of 98.79%, far above the 89.77% recorded in 2021. A total of 856 candidates contested 723 seats, and several prominent business and professional figures lost out in surprise upsets.

South Korean Education Minister Choi Kyo-jin resigned on Sunday, and President Lee Jae Myung accepted the resignation the same day. No. 15 · AI and Maths

Did AI Crack the Riemann Hypothesis? Anthropic Mathematician Says He Proved It With Claude

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge announced on November 20 that, working with Claude for about six weeks, he had completed a proof of the Riemann hypothesis — the problem posed in 1859 that has stood unsolved for nearly 170 years — releasing it as 17 million lines of Lean code. On November 21, the Lean and Mathlib communities said they had independently verified the proof; the Clay Mathematics Institute still lists it as unsolved, and peer review is yet to come.
